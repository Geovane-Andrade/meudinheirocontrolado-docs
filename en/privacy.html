Privacy Policy

MyMoneyUnderControl

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MyMoneyUnderControl app (the "App"), developed by Geovane de Andrade Grangeiro (the "Developer"), collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information Collected

MyMoneyUnderControl collects only the information necessary to provide its features.

Google Account Information

If you choose to sign in with a Google Account, the App may access:

  • Name;
  • Email address;
  • Google Account identifier.

These details are used only to identify the user and enable login, authentication, and backup features.

Financial Data

Users may voluntarily record information such as:

  • Income;
  • Expenses;
  • Categories;
  • Accounts;
  • Financial goals;
  • Recurrences;
  • Notes;
  • Other information entered by the user.

These data belong solely to the user and are used only to operate the App.

Backup Data

If backup is enabled, financial data may be stored in the user's own Google Drive account.

The Developer does not have access to the content stored in the user's Google Drive.

Technical Data

Technical information may also be collected to improve the App, including:

  • Device model;
  • Operating system version;
  • App version;
  • Crash reports;
  • Performance information.

These data are not used to personally identify users.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Enable the App's functionality;
  • Authenticate users;
  • Perform backups and restorations;
  • Synchronize data when requested;
  • Improve stability and performance;
  • Fix bugs;
  • Provide technical support.

The Developer does not sell, trade, or rent users' personal data.

3. Advertising

The free version of MyMoneyUnderControl may show ads provided by Google AdMob.

Google may collect certain information to deliver, measure, and improve ad delivery as described in its own Privacy Policy.

Where permitted by applicable law and your device or Google Account settings, advertising identifiers may be used to personalize ads.

You can manage ad preferences in your Google Account settings or your device privacy settings.

The Developer does not have access to the data Google uses to personalize ads and does not control which ads are shown.

4. Data Sharing

The Developer does not sell or share personal data with third parties, except when:

  • Required by law;
  • Necessary to comply with a court order;
  • Necessary for the operation of Google services such as Google Sign-In, Google Drive, and Google AdMob.

5. Data Storage

Financial data is stored locally on the user's device.

If backup is enabled, a copy may be stored in the user's Google Drive account.

Users are responsible for the security of their Google Account and the permissions they grant to the App.

6. Security

The Developer implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no system is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed against incidents or cyberattacks.

7. User Rights

Users may, at any time:

  • Delete data stored locally;
  • Revoke the permissions granted to the App;
  • Delete backup files from Google Drive;
  • Uninstall the App;
  • Request information about how their data is processed.

8. Children

MyMoneyUnderControl is not intended for children under 13 years old.

The Developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If inadvertent collection is identified, steps will be taken to delete such information.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes.

When significant changes occur, the update date of this document will be revised.

Continued use of the App after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

Data processing by MyMoneyUnderControl complies with Brazilian Law No. 13.709/2018 (General Personal Data Protection Law – LGPD).

Personal data may be processed based on the following legal bases, as applicable:

  • Performance of services requested by the user;
  • Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations;
  • User consent, when necessary;
  • The Developer's legitimate interest to ensure the App's security, stability, and continuous improvement, respecting users' rights.

Under the LGPD, users may request:

  • Confirmation of data processing;
