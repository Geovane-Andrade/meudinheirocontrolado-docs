1. Information Collected

MeuDinheiroControlado collects only the information necessary to provide its features.

Google Account Information

If you choose to sign in with a Google Account, the App may access:

  • Name;
  • Email address;
  • Google Account identifier.

This information is used only to identify the user and enable login, authentication, and backup features.

Google sign-in is used exclusively for user authentication and to enable features that depend on Google Account integration, such as data backup and restore. The App requests only the permissions strictly necessary for these features.

Signing in with Google is optional, except for features that depend on Google Account integration.

Financial Data

Users may voluntarily record information such as:

  • Income;
  • Expenses;
  • Categories;
  • Accounts;
  • Financial goals;
  • Recurring entries;
  • Notes;
  • Other user-entered information.

This data belongs exclusively to the user and is used only to operate the App.

Backup Data

If backup is enabled, financial data may be stored in the user's Google Drive account.

The Developer does not have access to the content stored in the user's Google Drive.

Access to Google Drive is used exclusively to create, list, restore, and delete backup files belonging to MeuDinheiroControlado. The App does not access, modify, or share other files stored in the user's Google Drive account.

Technical Data

Technical information may also be collected to improve the App, including:

  • Device model;
  • Operating system version;
  • App version;

This data is not used to personally identify users.

The information collected is used to:

The Developer does not sell or share personal data with third parties, except when:

5. Data Storage

Financial data is stored locally on the user's device.

If backup is enabled, a copy may be stored in the user's Google Drive account.

Users are responsible for the security of their Google Account and the permissions they grant to the App.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial data remains stored locally on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or until the App is uninstalled.

When backup is enabled, files remain stored only in the user's Google Drive account until the user decides to delete them manually.

The Developer does not keep copies of financial data on its own servers.

Users can delete their local data directly from the App, remove their Google Account, or delete backup files stored in their Google Drive at any time.
