Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to remove your account from the MeuDinheiroControlado application and delete the associated data.

How to remove your account

  1. Open the MeuDinheiroControlado app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Google Account.
  4. Tap Disconnect Account.

After disconnecting your Google Account, no further synchronization or backups will be performed.

How to delete your data

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • Delete all locally stored data using the option available in the app settings.
  • Uninstall the application to remove all data stored locally on your device.
  • Manually delete the backup files stored in your Google Drive account. Only the three most recent backups are kept in your Google Drive.

What data will be deleted

  • Financial transactions.
  • Accounts.
  • Categories.
  • Financial goals.
  • Recurring transactions.
  • All other data stored locally by the application.

What data may remain

Backup files stored in your Google Drive account will remain available until you manually delete them.

The Developer does not store copies of your financial data on its own servers.

Data retention period

Your data remains stored only until you delete it or uninstall the application. Backup files remain in your Google Drive account until you manually remove them.
