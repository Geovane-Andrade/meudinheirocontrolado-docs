Your privacy, in detail.

No hidden fine print. Open each section to see exactly what we do with your data.

01Collection and use of information
When you sign in with your Google Account, we collect only your email, name, and profile photo, used solely for identification within the app. For backup, we request Google Drive access exclusively to write and read your financial database backup file — no other files are read or modified.
02Storage and security
Your financial information is stored locally on your device and, when you choose to back it up, in your own Google Drive cloud storage. We do not use external servers: nothing is sold, shared, or sent to third parties or advertising networks.
04Data deletion
You can delete backup files at any time directly from your Google Drive, or revoke the app permission through Google Account Security Settings. Or directly in the app!
