About the app

MyMoneyUnderControl is a finance management app designed to help users track income, expenses, accounts, financial goals, and recurring transactions in a practical, organized, and secure way.

Financial data stays stored locally on the user's device and can be synced with their own Google Drive account through the backup feature.

Features

  • 📊 Income and expense tracking
  • 🏦 Account management
  • 🎯 Financial goals (COMING SOON)
  • 🔁 Recurring entries
  • ☁️ Google Drive backup
  • 🔐 Google sign-in
  • 📱 Local data storage
